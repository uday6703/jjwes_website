State-Level Children’s Day Celebration & Consultation with Children – Hyderabad

The Alliance for Child Rights (ACR) and MAHITA, with the support of UNICEF, in collaboration with Telangana State Commission for Protection of Child Rights (TGSCPCR), organized a State-Level Children’s Day Celebration and Consultation on 18 November 2026 at the WCD State Office, Hyderabad. Around 65 children from various districts of Telangana participated and shared their experiences, concerns and recommendations on education, health, safety, protection and welfare.

Key Discussions & Child Rights Priorities

TGSCPCR Members highlighted key child rights priorities, including:

  • Substance abuse prevention among children.
  • Support for children with special needs.
  • Prevention of teenage pregnancies.
  • Residential school infrastructure.
  • The role of parents and teachers in children's education.

Representatives from various government departments also emphasized interdepartmental coordination and responsive governance.

Children's Voices, Meaningful Action

The programme provided an important platform for children to interact directly with government officials and make their voices heard. Children shared their concerns, experiences and recommendations on issues affecting their lives. TGSCPCR assured appropriate follow-up on the issues raised by children, reinforcing the importance of child participation in governance and decision-making.
